# mod-105 — Champion Networks and Change Management for AI Adoption

**Estimated effort:** 18 hours
**Role level:** 45 · **Family:** AI Workforce

This is the module where the enablement program meets the workforce in both directions. Before the exec scorecard (mod-106), before the budget defense (mod-107), before industry-specific flavouring (mod-108), the role has to be able to design and run a champion network that carries the centre's content outward *and* surfaces field signal back inward — into policy (mod-103), tooling (mod-102), and training (mod-104). It has to run a rollout communications cadence that keeps the executive scorecard honest. It has to decide opt-in vs. mandate deliberately. It has to handle resistance patterns unique to AI adoption with established change-management frames adapted for the specifics. It has to measure its own health before business outcomes make the health question unavoidable. And it has to recognise when a change-management signal must leave this module and enter the governance-owned incident path (mod-109). Those are the moves; the eight chapters below are the operating manual.

## Learning objectives

- Design the champion network as a two-way feedback loop — recruit, train, recognise, retire — grounded in R8 (0.54, 14/26 postings); route champion field signal back into policy (mod-103), tooling choices (mod-102), and training content (mod-104).
- Run the rollout communications cadence that keeps the executive scorecard honest — what is being built, what is available, what is working, what employees should do next — grounded in R9 (0.69, 18/26 postings).
- Make the opt-in vs. mandate decision deliberately — the trade-offs, the signals that justify each, the organizational conditions under which one works and the other backfires.
- Handle resistance patterns unique to AI adoption — AI anxiety, role-threat narratives, "I already have ChatGPT at home," quality skepticism — using Prosci ADKAR and Kotter 8-step as frames adapted for AI-specific resistance.
- Measure change-management health — adoption curve shape, champion retention, communications reach, resistance survey — and act on each signal with a specific intervention.
- Coordinate the handoff from enablement to governance-owned incident path when a rollout surfaces harm (see mod-109).
