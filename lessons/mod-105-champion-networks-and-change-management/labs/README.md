# Labs for mod-105 — Champion Networks and Change Management for AI Adoption

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's [exercises](../exercises/): exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (champion-network design and recruit plan, two-way feedback-loop drill, cadence plan, opt-in/mandate decision memo, resistance playbook, health dashboard); labs operate one layer below on the specific plumbing those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Build the Field-Report Form and the 24-Hour Reply Macros](lab-01-build-the-field-report-form-and-reply-macros.md) — 60–75 min · author the six-field intake schema (with the `incident-shaped observation` tag wired to the Chapter 08 fast path and the anonymous-by-default operator field) plus five reply macros (POL / TOOL / TRAIN / INC / NEEDS-CLARIFY), red-teamed against Chapter 03's loop-breaking anti-patterns and exercised on one Exercise 02 report. Grounded in Chapter 03 (the one-pipe-three-routes design, the record-level loop close, the 24-hour reply commitment) and Chapter 02 (lifecycle filing cadence). Operates below [Exercise 02](../exercises/exercise-02-two-way-feedback-loop-drill.md), which assumes the form and macros already exist.
- [Lab 02 — Walk the Combined-Signal Dashboard Read](lab-02-walk-the-combined-signal-dashboard-read.md) — 60–75 min · diagnose three populated four-quadrant dashboard snapshots against Chapter 07's five named combined-signal reads, produce the named concern + named intervention + named owner per off-baseline quadrant, draft the exec-sponsor-facing scorecard paragraph with a specific ask, and red-team against the single-signal / interventionless / over-intervention failures. Grounded in Chapter 07 (the four signals, combined-signal reads, the dashboard discipline) and Chapter 06 (the resistance-pattern interventions the dashboard surfaces). Operates below [Exercise 06](../exercises/exercise-06-change-management-health-dashboard.md), which designs the dashboard template rather than reading it.
- [Lab 03 — Dry-Run the Incident-Path Handoff on Four Live Signals](lab-03-dry-run-the-incident-path-handoff.md) — 60–75 min · walk the four-step handoff (preserve, notify, transfer the facts, enter supporting mode) against four signal shapes — an `incident-shaped` field report, a dashboard adoption-up/skepticism-rising read, an external surface from a customer counsel, and a thin-narrative ambiguous report — producing the preservation note, the notification to the named governance-side owner, the facts-transfer memo, and the supporting-mode worklist for each, then red-teamed against Chapter 08's three boundary failures. Grounded in Chapter 08 (the four-step sequence, the decision table, the three boundary failures) and Chapter 03 (two of the four signals come in via the field-report pipe). Not covered by any module exercise.

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Lab 01's field-report form is the intake surface Lab 03's first and fourth signals arrive on; Lab 02's dashboard reads are the surface Lab 03's second signal is detected on. The three labs together exercise the plumbing beneath the module's six exercises and the mod-109 hand-off that Chapter 08 points at.
