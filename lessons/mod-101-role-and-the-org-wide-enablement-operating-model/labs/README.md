# Labs for mod-101 — The AI Enablement Lead: Role, Scope, and the Org-Wide Enablement Operating Model

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's exercises: exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (scope contract, CoE shape memo, handoff map, Article 4 drill, 90-day plan); labs operate one layer below on the specific plumbing those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Build the Decision-Rights Table, Row by Row](lab-01-build-the-decision-rights-table.md) — 60–75 min · a worked walkthrough of three rows, an independent build of the next five-to-seven, two organization-specific rows, and a red-team pass. Grounded in Chapter 03 (decision rights) and Chapter 01 (scope contract).
- [Lab 02 — Draft the CoE Service Catalog v0.1](lab-02-draft-the-service-catalog-v0-1.md) — 60 min · a structured build of the five audience segments Chapter 03 names (individuals, teams, BUs, functions, executive office), with deliverability / length / sunset cuts before versioning. Grounded in Chapter 03 (service catalog) and Chapter 02 (CoE shape).
- [Lab 03 — Dry-Run the Weekly Intake Triage](lab-03-dry-run-the-weekly-intake-triage.md) — 45–60 min · a mock Week-9 triage against six pre-written tickets covering all three intake queues (long-tail, pilot pipeline, incident fast lane), ending with a pattern scan and the weekly summary paragraph that feeds the Chapter 04 handoffs. Grounded in Chapter 03 (intake path) and Chapter 04 (handoffs).

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Each lab's outputs are useful inputs for the next: the decision-rights table is referenced during the triage lab, and the service catalog shapes which services tickets can be routed to.
