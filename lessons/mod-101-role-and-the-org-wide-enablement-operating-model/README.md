# mod-101 — The AI Enablement Lead: Role, Scope, and the Org-Wide Enablement Operating Model

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours
**Role level:** 45 · **Family:** AI Workforce

This module is the operating-system foundation for the rest of the track. Before you choose tools (mod-102), co-author policy (mod-103), build a training program (mod-104), or run a champion network (mod-105), you have to know what the role is, what shape of operating model you're running, and how its handoffs work. That is the whole module.

## Learning objectives

- Position the level-45 AI Enablement Lead role against `ai-augmented-team-manager` (L40), `head-of-ai-governance` (L60), `senior-ai-governance-architect` (L50), and `chief-ai-officer` (L70) — writing the one-page scope contract the role operates under.
- Read the AI Center of Excellence pattern family — hub, hub-and-spoke, federated, embedded — and choose a shape for a concrete organization with a defensible rationale.
- Author the enablement operating model — intake path, decision rights, funding model, staffing shape, service catalog.
- Diagram the handoffs with adjacent roles — `ai-governance-analyst` (L15), `ai-risk-engineer` (L25, peer), `ai-augmented-team-manager` (L40, below), `head-of-ai-governance` (L60, above).
- Read EU AI Act Article 4 (AI literacy) as the regulatory floor for the enablement operating model in EU-touching organizations.
- Position IAPP AIGP, ATD, and Prosci credentials as the shared vocabulary of the role; build a 90-day entry plan for the first time you hold the title.
