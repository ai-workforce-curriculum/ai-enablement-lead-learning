# Labs for mod-102 — Selecting and Rolling Out AI Tools at Organization Scale

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's exercises: exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (the three-stage selection one-pagers, the use-policy draft, the weekly executive report, the pilot charter, the full seven-artifact rollout bundle, and the outcome-vs-telemetry drill); labs operate one layer below on the meeting plumbing, measurement wiring, and facilitator mechanics those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Run the Three Stage-Gate Reviews on a Single Candidate](lab-01-run-the-three-stage-gate-reviews.md) — 75–90 min · build the facilitator pack for each of the three selection gates (attendees, the one question, go/no-go options, facilitator script, decision-line template, sign-off), then red-team the set. Grounded in Chapter 01 (three-stage selection) and Chapter 06 (peer handoffs at Gates 2 and 3).
- [Lab 02 — Wire the Per-Rollout Measurement Plan v0.1](lab-02-wire-the-measurement-plan-v0-1.md) — 60–75 min · a three-layer measurement plan (Layer 1 outcome metrics with primary+secondary, Layer 2 roles-×-BUs interview matrix with the verbatim four questions, Layer 3 three-metric telemetry panel), plus the readout rules and the explicit prohibition on citing telemetry as outcome. Grounded in Chapter 04 (measurement) and Chapter 02 (baselines).
- [Lab 03 — Facilitate the 45-Minute Pre-Mortem (Solo Dry-Run)](lab-03-facilitate-the-45-minute-pre-mortem.md) — 60 min · a solo dry-run of the Chapter 05 pre-mortem meeting (five attendees played in character, silent writing, verbatim capture, clustering against the four failure modes, artifact-and-owner assignment), producing a signed one-page pre-mortem and a short debrief. Grounded in Chapter 05 (pre-mortem), Chapter 03 (artifact set), and Chapter 01 (Stage 3 gate).

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. The stage-gate packs from Lab 01 reference the measurement plan from Lab 02 as a Stage 3 pre-read; Lab 03's pre-mortem is run at the end of Stage 3 as the prerequisite for the pilot-vs-rollout commit. Running the three in order gives you a coherent selection-through-commit flow for a single candidate.
