# Labs for mod-107 — Budget, ROI, and Build-vs-Buy at Organization Scope

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's [exercises](../exercises/): exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (the full six-family cost model, the three-year ROI projection, the four-column build-vs-buy matrix, the three-way partnership contract, the stack-consolidation case with sponsor memo); labs operate one layer below on the specific plumbing those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Build the Cost-Model Sourcing Map and the Quarterly Attestation Form](lab-01-build-the-cost-model-sourcing-map-and-attestation-form.md) — 60–75 min · produce the two authored artifacts that make the six-family cost model from [Chapter 01](../01-the-enablement-cost-model-at-org-scope.md) defensible across reorganizations, tool swaps, and staffing changes. Family-by-family sourcing block with authoritative source, reconciliation cadence, and named owner on the source side; cross-ledger reconciliation hooks (IT ticket AI-tag, legal/privacy/security AI-project code, HR enablement allocation) with explicit fallbacks; materiality threshold, fully-loaded FTE multiplier, and the AI-incremental cost rule with a worked example; the one-page quarterly attestation form for Family 3 SME time, Family 4 champion / shadow / governance-partner time, and Family 6 manager change-management load, with manager sign-off. Red-teamed against the three Chapter 01 sourcing disciplines and three of the five named undercounts. Grounded in Chapter 01 (the taxonomy, the sourcing map, the attestation rhythm) and [Chapter 04](../04-partnering-with-procurement-finance-and-it.md) (the Finance and IT ownership splits). Operates below [Exercise 01](../exercises/exercise-01-enablement-cost-model-drill.md), which populates the cost model itself; this lab builds the plumbing Exercise 01's methodology appendix points at by name.
- [Lab 02 — Compose the ROI Confidence Band via Monte Carlo](lab-02-compose-the-roi-confidence-band-via-monte-carlo.md) — 75–90 min · run a working composition of the four [Chapter 02](../02-projecting-roi-with-confidence-bands.md) term distributions (magnitude, timing, attribution, cost) into a composed three-year ROI distribution. Author each term's `p10 / p50 / p90` with named grounding sources (trailing measurement, literature, vendor-upper-bound, attestation); sample 1,000 draws per term via triangular (or normal where symmetric) distributions with a recorded seed; compose each draw into a three-year ROI using a J-curve-aware gross-value model; read the central estimate and 90% band off the sample; sensitivity-re-run per term to rank contribution to band width; record the result in the Chapter 02 canonical three-sentence form. Red-teamed against Chapter 02's three projection-specific theatre patterns (hockey-stick, walking band, attribution by assertion). Grounded in Chapter 02 (the four dimensions, the J-curve, the two composition approaches) and Chapter 01 (Family 1-6 cost totals as the cost-term grounding). Operates below [Exercise 02](../exercises/exercise-02-roi-projection-with-confidence-bands.md), which carries the projection narrative; this lab computes the band the narrative quotes. The same composition pattern feeds [Exercise 03](../exercises/exercise-03-org-scope-build-vs-buy-matrix.md)'s per-candidate ROI bands and [Exercise 05](../exercises/exercise-05-tool-stack-consolidation-worked-example.md)'s consolidation-case band.
- [Lab 03 — Fill in the One-Page Vendor-Vetting Rubric for One Specific Vendor](lab-03-fill-in-the-vendor-vetting-rubric.md) — 45–60 min · operationalize [Chapter 06](../06-the-vendor-vetting-rubric.md)'s rubric for one specific current-market vendor (GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, Microsoft Copilot, Glean, Harvey, Nuance DAX, Ada, Gong, etc.) that the organization is piloting, renewing, or consolidating with. Context block naming use case, deployment scope, decision pending, and which of the four Chapter 06 triggers fired; score and 2-3 sentence note on each of the six dimensions (capability trajectory, data and model posture, security and compliance, commercial resilience, operating / admin surface, switching and exit) with open items routed to named resolution owners; summary block with a shape-conveying posture sentence, specific red flags, scheduled next review date; portfolio concentration paragraph running the vendor through Chapter 06's three concentration questions. Red-teamed against Chapter 06's three failure modes (polished-pitch illusion, security-only review, stale score) with a calendar reminder set for the next review date. Grounded in Chapter 06 (the rubric, the four triggers, the three failure modes, the concentration view), [Chapter 03](../03-the-build-vs-buy-matrix-at-enterprise-scope.md) (the vendor-concentration and capability-ceiling dimensions in the build-vs-buy matrix), and [Chapter 04](../04-partnering-with-procurement-finance-and-it.md) (the contract-term vocabulary scored in Dimensions 2 and 3). Not covered by any module exercise — Chapter 06 is deliberately proportional to R17 at 0.23, with no exercise of its own; this lab is where the chapter becomes a running operating instrument. The rubric produced here feeds Exercise 03's vendor-concentration dimension, Exercise 05's vendor-concentration check, and [Exercise 04](../exercises/exercise-04-procurement-and-finance-partnership-contract.md)'s renewal-plus-90 interface.

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Lab 01 builds the plumbing under the cost model (the denominator the ROI numerator from Lab 02 is divided by); Lab 02 composes the confidence band over that denominator and the projected numerator; Lab 03 fills in the vendor lens that sits alongside both when a specific vendor is the subject of a build-vs-buy or consolidation decision. Together the three labs exercise the budget, ROI, and vendor-vetting plumbing beneath Exercises 01, 02, 03, and 05; [Exercise 04](../exercises/exercise-04-procurement-and-finance-partnership-contract.md)'s three-way partnership contract then references the sourcing map (Lab 01), the band-composition artifact (Lab 02), and the vendor rubric (Lab 03) as the three operating artifacts the contract's Finance, IT, and Procurement sections point to by name.
