# Labs for mod-106 — Outcome Measurement and Executive Reporting

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's [exercises](../exercises/): exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (the per-rollout outcome framework, the weekly rollup, the quarterly review outline, the miss-narrative drill, the literature-to-metric map, the ROI defence); labs operate one layer below on the specific plumbing those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Author the Analyst-Ready Metric Specification (Specs, Not Names)](lab-01-author-the-analyst-ready-metric-specification.md) — 60–75 min · produce the metric-specification sheet the `ai-augmented-analyst` builds the dashboard against for one rollout: the specification paragraph (not the name) answering what, events, grain, inclusion filter, outliers, cadence; the cohort definition; the baseline with named provenance; the confidence-band construction method and what the band does *not* cover; the three Chapter 02 annotations (metric definition, baseline provenance, confidence framing) pulled into one auditor-ready block; priority tier and dependencies; and SQL-level pseudocode. Red-teamed against the four Chapter 01 theatre patterns at the specification layer and the two Chapter 06 role-boundary failures. Grounded in Chapter 06 (the analyst interface), Chapter 01 (the Layer 1 outcome discipline), and Chapter 02 (the three auditable annotations). Operates below [Exercise 01](../exercises/exercise-01-outcome-vs-usage-metric-framework.md), which produces the framework the rollout owner fills in; this lab produces the analyst-ready specification that sits between the framework and the dashboard.
- [Lab 02 — Build the Role × BU × Tool Interview Coverage Matrix and the Quarter's Interview Roster](lab-02-build-the-interview-coverage-matrix-and-the-quarters-roster.md) — 60–75 min · operate the Chapter 01 Layer 2 qualitative-ground-truth discipline as a calendar with names on it: the portfolio snapshot; the coverage matrix with explicit labels (monthly / quarterly / em-dash / GAP / ad-hoc); per-GAP diagnosis with rollout, days-since-GA, reason, severity, and action; the quarter's signed roster (week, rollout, BU, role, interviewer, interviewee count, four-question prompt, transcript owner) with flagged-gap closures and an explicit "will-not-happen" footer; the four Chapter 01 interview questions verbatim; and the anonymization-at-ingest protocol across interview, transcription, aggregation, retention, and failure. Red-teamed against the three qualitative-discipline rules and the three Chapter 06 data-governance surfaces (personal data, cross-BU access, vendor telemetry). Grounded in Chapter 01 (the coverage matrix, the four questions, the three qualitative-ground-truth rules) and Chapter 06 (the team-manager interface and the three data-governance surfaces). Produces the qualitative input that Exercise 02's Section 2 and Exercise 03's Section 5 assume already exists.
- [Lab 03 — Red-Team Three Executive Paragraphs Against the Theatre Patterns and the Structural Moves](lab-03-red-team-three-executive-paragraphs-against-the-theatre-patterns.md) — 60–75 min · diagnose and rewrite three short failing executive paragraphs — a board-update opening (engagement lede + vendor voice + strategy abstraction), an LT-briefing BLUF (quiet strategy abstraction and missing BLUF loads), and a strategic memo to the CFO on a drafting-workflow miss (euphemism + vendor-number headline + strategy abstraction + absent program-level *learned* line). Each round runs diagnosis → rewrite → self-red-team against Chapter 03's three theatre failure modes and three structural moves (BLUF, misses-in-the-lead, confidence bands), with cross-references into Chapter 02's miss-narrative discipline and Chapter 05's reverse-engineered-ROI pattern where they apply. Grounded in Chapter 03 (the three artifacts, three structural moves, three theatre patterns, drafting rhythm) and Chapter 02 (the five-part miss narrative, the misses-first discipline). Not covered by any module exercise — the exercises produce positive output; this lab drills the diagnostic reflex on drafts that have already failed.

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Lab 01 pins down the outcome metric at specification depth; Lab 02 populates the qualitative layer that cuts against or sharpens the Lab 01 metric when the two disagree; Lab 03 is the executive-writing drill that turns the combined Lab 01 + Lab 02 output into a paragraph a board, an LT, or a CFO can act on without theatre. Together the three labs exercise the measurement-and-reporting plumbing beneath Exercises 01, 02, 03, and 04; Exercise 05's literature map and Exercise 06's ROI defence then compose onto the specification (Lab 01), the attribution interviews (Lab 02), and the executive-communication craft (Lab 03) the labs have built.
