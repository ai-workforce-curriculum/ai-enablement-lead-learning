# Labs for mod-108 — Industry and Regulatory Flavours of Enablement

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's [exercises](../exercises/): exercises produce the big-picture industry-adjusted artifacts (Article 4 training-content drill, posting-to-obligation map, responsibility-split RACI with drift memos, industry-adjusted policy package, industry-adjusted training curriculum); labs operate one layer below on the specific sentence-, triage-, and pre-read-level plumbing those artifacts depend on.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Author the Article 4 Three-Tier Proportionality Rationale](lab-01-author-the-article-4-proportionality-rationale.md) — 60–75 min · draft the one-to-two-sentence proportionality rationale per Article 4 tier (leadership / operator / specialist), red-team each against the three distortions Chapter 02 names (marketing e-learning, blanket-engineering-course, literacy-as-communications), cross-tier consistency pass, debrief. Grounded in Chapter 02 (the three tiers, the written-rationale sentence, the three distortions) with Chapter 01 as the shape-vs-content frame. Operates below [Exercise 01](../exercises/exercise-01-eu-ai-act-article-4-training-content-drill.md), which asks for the whole tier outline across five required sections including the rationale sentence as one of them.
- [Lab 02 — Dry-run the "Is It a Model?" Intake Triage](lab-02-dry-run-the-is-it-a-model-intake-triage.md) — 75–90 min · triage three bank-flavoured candidate-tool intake submissions (marketing drafter, credit-memo narrative drafter, call-transcript summarizer) through the Chapter 04 three sub-questions; produce the routing packet to MRM; draft operator-facing holding responses in Chapter 08's operator-surfaces-a-question posture; debrief against the drifted-sentence patterns. Grounded in Chapter 04 (the "is it a model?" question and its three sub-questions, intake deltas), Chapter 07 (the finance overlay and the three-lines-of-defense test), and Chapter 08 (Posture 1 sentence patterns). Operates below [Exercise 04](../exercises/exercise-04-industry-adjusted-policy-package.md), which produces the intake-questionnaire delta — the lab simulates the week-in-week-out triage work that runs that delta on live submissions.
- [Lab 03 — Author the One-Page Pre-Read for an Industry Counsel Meeting](lab-03-author-the-one-page-pre-read-for-an-industry-counsel-meeting.md) — 60–75 min · pick one specific conversation (healthcare privacy officer / finance MRM lead / legal engineering general counsel / ed-tech chief privacy officer) and author the Chapter 08 one-page pre-read (operational situation / specific decisions needed / timing signal) plus attached operational context, proposed structure for the answer, explicit non-asks, and self-check against the drifted-sentence patterns. Grounded in Chapter 08 (the pre-read discipline, the three postures, the sentence patterns that stay in scope, the four questions that signal drift) with the chosen industry chapter as the content anchor. Operates below [Exercise 03](../exercises/exercise-03-enablement-vs-governance-responsibility-split-in-industry.md) (the RACI shows *which* counterpart owns an obligation; the lab's pre-read is how the enablement lead actually *runs the conversation* with that counterpart) and complements [Exercise 04](../exercises/exercise-04-industry-adjusted-policy-package.md) (the policy-package deliverables are the artifact-level consequence of the decisions the pre-reads surface).

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Lab 01 builds the regulator-voice sentence-level muscle for the horizontal Article 4 obligation before Exercises 01 and 05 need it in multiple places. Lab 02 builds the finance-overlay intake-triage muscle that composes with Exercise 04's intake-questionnaire delta. Lab 03 builds the industry-counsel pre-read discipline that runs against every counterpart Exercise 03's RACI names. The three labs together exercise the full plumbing beneath the module's five exercises — sentence-authoring, triage, and conversation — without duplicating what the exercises produce.
