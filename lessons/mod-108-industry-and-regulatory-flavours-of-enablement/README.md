# mod-108-industry-and-regulatory-flavours-of-enablement: Industry and Regulatory Flavours of Enablement

> Scaffolded by `aicg org execute-plan`. Lecture chapters and exercise content are authored on subsequent autonomous cycles.

**Estimated effort:** 14 hours

## Learning objectives

- Translate the EU AI Act Article 4 AI-literacy obligation for deployers into concrete training-program content — who must be trained on what by when — for EU-touching organizations
- Read the industry-specific flavours the enablement lead meets in sampled postings — healthcare (HIPAA; p-018 Navitus Health Solutions, p-008 WelbeHealth), finance (FINRA / SR 11-7 enablement slice; p-014 Wells Fargo AI Enablement Lending Lead, p-012 Dodge & Cox, p-022 Common Securitization Solutions), legal engineering (p-021 Disney legal AI), education (p-009 ed-tech, p-013 Ridgeline, p-017 McGraw Hill) — grounded in R19 (0.27, 7/26 postings, below-threshold but material in-industry)
- Position the enablement-side obligations under each regulator separate from the control-ownership obligations owned by `head-of-ai-governance` (level 60) and `senior-ai-governance-architect` (level 50)
- Partner with industry-specific legal and compliance counterparts without taking legal positions — the enablement lead does not deliver legal opinion
- Author the industry-adjusted version of the mod-103 policy package and the mod-104 training curriculum for one in-scope industry (learner's choice)
