# Labs for mod-103 — Policy Co-Authoring with Governance, Legal, Security, and Privacy

Guided, step-by-step hands-on practice. Labs complement — do not duplicate — the module's exercises: the exercises produce the big-picture artifacts (acceptable-use policy, data-handling SOP, intake submission + architect memo, council pre-read, standards-mapping table + gaps memo); the labs operate one layer below on the specific plumbing those artifacts depend on — the walking-redline slot that generates the redline log, the 20-minute pre-review session that reshapes a request before submission, and the route-out drift drill that keeps the three handoffs (counsel, SOC, architect) from collapsing into one person's job.

## Available labs

- [Lab 01 — Run a Walking-Redline Slot and Build the Redline Log](lab-01-run-a-walking-redline-slot-and-build-the-redline-log.md) — 60–75 min · a solo dry-run of a 30-minute walking-redline slot against an AUP or SOP draft, in character as one named governance partner (counsel, CISO delegate, or DPO), producing the three-to-eight redline-log rows Chapter 01 names as per-slot output. Grounded in Chapter 01 (co-author posture and the redline log) with Chapters 04 and 05 on the drafts being redlined.
- [Lab 02 — Run the 20-Minute Intake Pre-Review Session](lab-02-run-the-twenty-minute-intake-pre-review-session.md) — 45 min · a solo dry-run of the pre-review session Chapter 06 names as "the preempt against formality," played in character as both the enablement runner and the BU-side requester, producing the reshape table and the three-sentence pre-review summary that Exercise 03's submission relies on. Grounded in Chapter 06 (the intake questionnaire) with Chapters 02 and 07 on the council-side consumption of the submission and the discipline that keeps the session on the runner side of the schema-vs-runner line.
- [Lab 03 — The Route-Out Drift Drill](lab-03-the-route-out-drift-drill.md) — 60–75 min · a solo drill that classifies a twelve-item corpus against the three drifts (legal opinion, SOC-side incident handling, control-library architecture), rewrites every drifted item to stay on the enablement side with the operational knowledge preserved, and builds the three handoff-mechanics artifacts (legal review slot + question queue, enablement-side evidence-pack SLA, quarterly architect evidence memo) Chapter 07 names. Grounded in Chapter 07 (what you route out) with Chapters 01, 04, 05, and 06 as the artifact surfaces the drifts most often occur against.

## Recommended order

Lab 01 → Lab 02 → Lab 03. Lab 01 builds the artifact (the redline log) that evidences every co-authored policy the module produces; Lab 02 builds the intake-side muscle that keeps the governance-council pipeline full without rubber-stamped submissions; Lab 03 is the discipline that stops all of the above from collapsing into one seat's workload as the role matures. Pairing each lab with its carried-forward tool-and-organization from Exercises 01–04 produces a coherent set of artifacts — redline log + pre-review session + handoff mechanics — that sit cleanly alongside the AUP, SOP, intake submission, council pre-read, and standards-mapping memo the exercises produce.
